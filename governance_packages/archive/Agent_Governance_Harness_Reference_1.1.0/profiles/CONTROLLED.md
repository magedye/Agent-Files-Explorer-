# CONTROLLED

Apply to the scoped high-impact boundary: production/privilege change, protected data processing/egress, destructive or irreversible effects, or an applicable obligation for independent review. This is not permission to perform those operations.

Specify exact actor/resource/environment, trusted grant, data route, protected policy and approval process, rollback or compensating action, observable intent/result and incident owner. Qualify actual prevention at every relevant route, including shell/delegation/server tools. Unknown critical protection is a blocker.

Before release/activation, bind evidence to the real candidate and environment, verify recovery if relied on, and obtain the required independent decision. Record residual accepted limitations explicitly. Continue unrelated low-risk authorized work under its own scope instead of imposing controlled ceremony on every keystroke.
