# Evidence-led review — no edits by default

Review the proposed change against the original request, actual governing sources, protected constraints and the
exact subject revision/worktree. Declare reviewer identity and evidence of separation. Use SELF_REVIEW when you
implemented the work; a new session or different model alone does not establish independent authorization.

Inspect the diff, relevant tests, raw results and requirement coverage. Check missing source conditions, changed
acceptance/tests, stale or fabricated-looking evidence, unintended files, dependency/network effects and false
completion. Treat uncertainty as uncertainty; do not convert lack of evidence to success or automatic defect.

Report findings with impact, concrete evidence, affected requirement, correction and how to verify it. Separate
blocking sensitive issues from nonblocking improvements. For ordinary local work do not invent a mandatory review
committee; for higher risk respect the real owner policy and runtime qualification requirements.

Return a scoped review verdict, not owner acceptance or deployment permission. Do not edit, install, push or deploy
unless separately requested. No finding is an implementation authorization merely because it is actionable.
