## 0. Root-Core qualification
The candidate must remain single across `أَمِن / أمن / أمنة / أمين / أمانة / اؤتمن / مأمن / مأمون / آمَن / إيمان / مؤمن`; must not contain Form IV or particle contribution; must survive objective-safe and false-security contexts; must remain distinct from nearby Quranic lexemes; must be falsifiable.

## 1. Root Core

### Formal
> **أ م ن: حالة علائقية يسقط فيها التحفظ والتوقّي تجاه جهة أو حال، ضمن نطاق محدد.**

### Operational
> تتحقق أ م ن عندما لا تُعامَل الجهة أو الحال في نطاق العلاقة على نحو يستدعي الاحتراز والتوقّي منها، سواء وافقت هذه الحالة الواقع الخارجي أم لم توافقه.

### Conceptual structure
`طرف ↔ سقوط تحفظ/توقٍ في نطاق محدد ↔ جهة/حال`

### Important constraint
The core is not equivalent to objective external safety. `أفأمنوا مكر الله` and `تأمنه... لا يؤده` allow AMN-state attribution without guaranteeing external safety/performance.

## 2. Semantic boundary

### REQUIRED
- a scoped relation/state;
- a party or condition toward which precaution/guarding is meaningful;
- realization of reduced/absent precaution in that scope.

### PERMITTED local realizations
- absence of fear;
- entrustment/delegation;
- reliance;
- acceptance of a report in a reporting context;
- leaving extra guarantee/security measures;
- spatial/environmental security;
- security that matches reality or does not.

### EXCLUDED AS SHARED ROOT CORE
- `تصديق` alone;
- `صدق`;
- `طمأنينة`;
- `حفظ`;
- fulfillment/faithfulness;
- guarantee/documentation;
- objective safety alone;
- absence of fear alone;
- mere mental knowledge;
- tawhid/shirk/islam as imported concepts.

### UNKNOWN
- whether `سقوط التحفظ والتوقي` is the final optimal abstraction;
- final distinction from ص د ق / و ث ق / ط م ن / ح ف ظ / ي ق ن / س ل م;
- Quran-wide operator value of Form IV;
- Quran-wide contribution of ب/ل.

## 5. Morphology and constructions

### أَمِنَ
> تحقق للفاعل حالة أ م ن تجاه جهة/حال محددة.

### آمَنَ — ROOT_INTERNAL candidate
> أقام/أحدث الفاعل تحقق حالة أ م ن; construction distributes the participant roles.

This is **not** yet a Quran-wide Form IV law.

### آمَنَ + direct object
> direct object bears the induced AMN state. Example: `آمنهم`.

### آمَنَ بـ X — LOCAL_CONSTRUCTION candidate
> X is the relation's target/anchor-like relatum; do not generalize `ب = ANCHOR` Quran-wide yet.

### آمَنَ لـ X — LOCAL_CONSTRUCTION candidate
> X is the direction/recipient of the AMN treatment/effect; in reporting contexts a local realization may approximate granting report-source reliability.

### مؤمن
Name/agent analysis must preserve a contribution that can hold when the form is applied to God; it cannot require ignorance→belief transition.

### إيمان
Source of Form IV; event/state/continuing-relation prominence remains context-sensitive. It is not formally identical to `الأمن`.
