## 0. Root-Core qualification
The candidate must cross nominal patterns and Forms II/IV/X without gloss collapse; must not invent an unattested base verb; must explain entity and relational domains; must not reduce to military peace/greeting/submission; must survive the hard branch `سُلَّم`; and must remain distinct from AMN and other neighbors.

## 1. Root Core candidate

### Formal
> **س ل م: تحقُّق كيان أو علاقة على وجهٍ لا يداخله مؤثرٌ مناقض أو منازع يخلّ تمامه أو يعوق جريانه/تحققه على وجهه.**

### Operational
> يتحقق س ل م عندما تقوم البنية أو العلاقة على وجه غير مختل أو متنازع عليه بمؤثر يناقض تمامها أو جريانها.

### Conceptual structure
`كيان/علاقة ↔ تحقق غير مختل/غير منازَع بمؤثر معطّل ↔ تمام/جريان العلاقة أو الكيان`

### Current epistemic note
This formulation improves on the earlier purely privative `absence of obstacle` core, but remains a candidate because Form II residue, Form X, `سُلَّم`, and lexical differentiation remain open.

## 2. Boundary

### REQUIRED candidate
- entity or relation capable of interference/opposition/impairment;
- realization without an effective contradicting/competing factor that disrupts its integrity/course.

### PERMITTED local realizations
- intactness/soundness;
- cessation of active conflict;
- non-contested relation;
- non-resistance in a scoped relation;
- greeting/discourse realization of a non-hostile relation;
- ability without an impairing condition;
- transfer/handing realization in Form II if independently demonstrated.

### EXCLUDED AS SHARED ROOT CORE
- military peace alone;
- greeting alone;
- `استسلام` as complete identity;
- submission/obedience as imported glosses;
- bodily safety alone;
- non-harm alone;
- AMN/imān;
- voluntariness as required feature of `أَسْلَمَ`;
- the full system-level Quranic concept named `الإسلام` as if all its content were contained in the root core.

### UNKNOWN / OPEN
- final positive residue wording;
- exact Form II contribution;
- exact Form X contribution;
- `سُلَّم` bridge;
- full discourse `سلام` corpus;
- SLM↔AMN semantic residue;

- `ل` vs `إلى` constructions with `أسلم`.

## 5. Morphological/form-stratified analysis

### Important absence
No unattested Form-I gloss may be invented. Root Core is inferred from attested family evidence.

### أَسْلَمَ — ROOT_INTERNAL candidate
> أقام الفاعل نفسه أو ما يتعدى إليه في علاقة س ل م بالنسبة إلى جهة، بحيث لا تبقى من جانبه منازعة/ممانعة فعالة تناقض تحقق تلك العلاقة.

This is a root-internal Form IV candidate, not yet a Quran-wide Form IV law.

### سلَّم / تسليم — OPEN
Requires independent Form II corpus. `4:65` proves non-redundancy beyond `لا حرج`, but does not by itself assign the residue to Root Core.

### استسلم — OPEN
Form X must be analyzed independently; it must not be used as a gloss-equivalent of `أَسْلَمَ`.

### سَلَم / سِلْم / سَلام / سُلَّم
One root corpus by default, but strictly stratified by nominal pattern under FORM–MEANING COVARIANCE before transferring pattern-specific evidence.

### `السلام` as discourse expression
Candidate local realization: presentation/establishment of a non-contesting/non-hostile relation. Requires full internal corpus before finalization.

### `سُلَّم`
Current status: `HARD_DERIVATION / INDETERMINATE-COMPATIBLE`. Context associates it with access/connection across separation; bridge to Root Core remains unresolved.

## 6. Meaning of أَسْلَمَ and الإسلام

### أَسْلَمَ
> **إقامة الذات/المتأثر في علاقة س ل م بحيث لا تبقى من جانبه ممانعة فعالة تناقض تحقق العلاقة في نطاقها.**

### `أَسْلَمَ لِـ...`
`ل` contribution remains local-construction candidate; do not import a Quran-wide particle value yet.

### الإسلام — derivational minimum
> **مصدر أَسْلَمَ؛ يدل اشتقاقيًا على تحقق/إقامة علاقة أَسْلَمَ من جهة الفاعل.**

The broader Quranic lexeme/topic `الإسلام` in constructions such as `الدين ... الإسلام` requires its own lexeme-level corpus and must not be equated automatically with every detail of the root/form definition.
