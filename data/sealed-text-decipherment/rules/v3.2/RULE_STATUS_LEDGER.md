## E. Proposed refinement/generalization

### LINGUISTIC OPERATOR INVARIANCE / OPERATOR-CONTRIBUTION INDEPENDENCE & INVARIANCE
Status: `INTEGRATED_AS_R111_WITH_SCOPE_LABELING`.

Principle: recurring linguistic operators are investigated under a one-invariant-contribution hypothesis. This generalizes the existing morphological invariance principle toward particles and recurrent constructions.

Critical separation:
- **operator invariance** = methodological principle;
- **concrete operator value** = empirical hypothesis requiring evidence.

Therefore the following are NOT established laws:
- `أَفْعَلَ = CAUSE/ESTABLISH`
- `ب = ANCHOR`
- `ل = DIRECTION`
